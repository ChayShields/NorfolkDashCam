# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4, deployed to Vercel. Client's standing stack choice across all site builds.

## Users

Two audiences:
1. Casual social-media visitors arriving mostly from Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok on mobile devices, looking to watch/follow Norfolk dashcam content or submit their own footage.
2. Local Norfolk business owners evaluating whether to advertise across Norfolk Dash Cam's social media platforms.

## Product Purpose

Norfolk Dash Cam is a social-media brand that shares dashcam footage of road incidents from Norfolk, UK, across Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok. The website exists to establish a professional, credible home base for the brand and drive three outcomes: visitors submitting their own dashcam footage, visitors following the brand's social channels, and local businesses enquiring about advertising.

## Positioning

An established, active Norfolk-specific dashcam/road-incident content brand with a real audience across four social platforms and a working footage-submission pipeline — not a generic template site.

## Operating Context

- Footage submission happens off-site via a third-party partner platform (partners.swd.media), not on this website.
- Advertising enquiries route to WhatsApp (07356 290069 / wa.me/447356290069) or a Jotform sponsorship request form.
- No CMS. No backend. Static/marketing site only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No footage upload, playback, or hosting functionality lives on this site — submission and advertising enquiries both hand off to external services.
- No blog/CMS is planned.
- All outbound links (submission partner, social platforms, WhatsApp, Jotform, developer credit) open in a new tab.
- Mobile-first is a hard requirement — a significant share of traffic arrives from social platforms on mobile.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Norfolk Dash Cam.
- Existing visual identity: circular camera-badge logo and a yellow/black caution-stripe motoring banner graphic (see `public/brand/`), establishing a bold, high-contrast, road/motoring aesthetic as the anchor for the site's visual world.
- Social handles: Facebook (facebook.com/p/Norfolk-Dash-Cam-100063704434791), YouTube (youtube.com/c/NorfolkDashCam), Instagram (@norfolkdashcam), TikTok (@norfolkdashcam).
- Footer must carry "Designed & Developed by Chay Shields" linking to hireme.link, subtle and positioned at the bottom — standing requirement across the client's site builds.

## Evidence on Hand

- Full written design/development spec supplied by the client (Google Doc), covering navigation, page structure, copy direction for hero/about/submit/social/advertising sections, and all required external links.
- Two brand image assets extracted from that spec, stored at `public/brand/logo-badge.png` and `public/brand/banner-reference.png`.
- No testimonials, press mentions, follower counts, or footage examples were supplied — none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Every page keeps visitors one tap from the two things that matter: submitting footage and following the socials.
- Mobile is the primary experience, not an adaptation of desktop.
- The advertising section must read as a distinct, credible B2B pitch, not an afterthought bolted onto a fan-content site.
- Visual identity extends the existing brand assets (yellow/black, road/motoring, bold type) rather than introducing a new unrelated look.
- No invented content — copy stays within what the client's spec and assets actually support.
